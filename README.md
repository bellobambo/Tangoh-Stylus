# 🏗️ TangoH Smart Contract


**Contract Address:** `0xcDFb1272Fad230337C553e8c5649d5C5cf361f03`  
**Network:** Arbitrum Stylus

## 📖 Overview

This smart contract is the trustworthy backbone of TangoH, ensuring every student's voice is permanently recorded and impossible to ignore. It transforms scattered complaints into organized, trackable issues that campus authorities must address. Think of it as an unchangeable public record where every campus problem gets documented and prioritized by the student community.

## 🎯 What It Solves

**For Students:**
- No more lost emails or ignored complaints
- A fair way to show which issues affect the most people
- Proof that your concerns were officially reported
- Ability to track resolution progress transparently

**For Campus Authorities:**
- Clear, prioritized list of what needs fixing
- Direct feedback from the student body
- Way to demonstrate accountability and responsiveness
- Organized system for managing campus improvements

## ✨ Key Features

### 📝 Simple Issue Reporting
Straight forward issue reporting for any campus problem - broken facilities, safety concerns, maintenance issues, or other concerns. Every report gets a permanent ID that can't be deleted.

### 🤝 Community Voting System
Students vote on issues they also experience. Problems with the most votes automatically rise to the top, giving authorities clear guidance on priorities.

### 🔄 Transparent Tracking
Watch issues move from "reported" to "in progress" to "resolved" with full visibility. No more wondering if your complaint was received or forgotten.

### ✅ Resolution Confirmation
When authorities fix a problem, the student who reported it can confirm the solution actually worked, closing the feedback loop.

## 🛡️ Built on Trust

- **Permanent Records**: Once reported, issues can't be deleted or altered
- **Fair for Everyone**: Every student gets equal voting power
- **Public Visibility**: Anyone can see what issues exist and their status
- **Tamper-Proof**: Built on blockchain technology for maximum reliability

## 🚀 How It Works

1. **Report**: Student submits an issue 
2. **Vote**: Other students/excos support issues they've seen too
3. **Prioritize**: Most-voted issues get attention first
4. **Resolve**: Authorities work on solutions and update status
5. **Confirm**: Student verifies the issue is actually fixed

## 💡 Why It Matters

This isn't just technology - it's a better way for students and campus authorities to work together. It replaces frustration with collaboration, complaints with solutions, and silence with productive conversation.
